# Career conversation with a mentee

**Use when:** a mentee or report wants to talk about where they're headed

**You'll need:** what they've told you about goals, strengths, frustrations

## Prompt

```text
Help me run a career conversation with [NAME], a [ROLE/LEVEL].

What they've said about their goals: [NOTES]
Strengths I've seen: [NOTES]
Gaps I've seen: [NOTES]

1. 5 open questions that help them figure out what they want, not what I'd want for them.
2. How their goals map to real paths (IC track, management, specialization, a different role) and the evidence each path would need.
3. One stretch opportunity I could realistically offer in the next quarter.
4. How to give the gap feedback honestly without making it the whole conversation.

Coach, don't solve. Give me the questions more than the answers.
```
