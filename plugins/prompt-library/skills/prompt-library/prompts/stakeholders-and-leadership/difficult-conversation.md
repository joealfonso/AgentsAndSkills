# Difficult conversation prep

**Use when:** conflict, delivering hard feedback, or a conversation you've been avoiding

**You'll need:** the situation, the person, what you want to change

## Prompt

```text
Help me prepare for a hard conversation with [PERSON / ROLE].

Situation: [FACTS, specific events and dates]
What I'm feeling and assuming (keep separate from facts): [NOTES]
What I want to change: [OUTCOME]

1. Separate facts from my interpretations in what I wrote.
2. The opening: two sentences that name the issue directly and invite their view.
3. Likely reactions (defensive, surprised, agreeing too fast) and how to respond to each.
4. What a good outcome looks like and how to agree on a concrete next step.
5. What I should not say.

Be honest if my part in the situation is bigger than I've described.
```
