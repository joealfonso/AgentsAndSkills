# Stakeholder map for a new initiative

**Use when:** starting a project with many people who can help or block it

**You'll need:** the initiative, the people involved

## Prompt

```text
Map the stakeholders for [INITIATIVE].

People involved: [NAME, ROLE, what I know about their goals and stance]

For each: what they care about, what they could block, what they need from this project, current stance (supporter / neutral / skeptic / unknown), and how often and how to keep them in the loop.

Then: the 2 people whose support matters most, who I haven't talked to but should, and the first conversation to have this week with the question I should open with.
```
