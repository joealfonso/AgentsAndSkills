# Executive summary (BLUF)

**Use when:** you need to turn a long doc, thread or project into something an exec reads in 60 seconds

**You'll need:** the source material, the audience, the decision you need

## Prompt

```text
Write an executive summary of the material below for [AUDIENCE].

The decision or support I need from them: [ASK, or "FYI only"]
Material:
[PASTE]

Format:
- First line: the ask or the conclusion, in one sentence.
- 3-5 short lines: why, the key evidence, the main risk, the cost of waiting.
- Options if there's a decision, with my recommendation marked.
- Under 150 words total.

Translate design terms into business consequences. Cut anything that doesn't change their decision.
```
