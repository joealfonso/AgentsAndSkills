# Decision memo

**Use when:** a decision is contested or will be questioned later, and you want it written down properly

**You'll need:** the decision, options, evidence, who decides

## Prompt

```text
Write a decision memo.

Decision to make: [QUESTION]
Options: [LIST]
Evidence and constraints: [PASTE]
Decider: [NAME/ROLE]. Deadline: [DATE].

Sections: context in 3 sentences, options with honest pros and cons (including the do-nothing option), recommendation with the reasoning, what we're giving up, risks and how we'd detect them early, and what would make us revisit.

Steelman every option I don't recommend. If the evidence doesn't support my preferred option, say so.
```
