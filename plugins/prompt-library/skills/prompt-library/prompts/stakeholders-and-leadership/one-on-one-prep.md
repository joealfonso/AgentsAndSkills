# 1:1 with my manager

**Use when:** regular 1:1s, so they cover what matters instead of status

**You'll need:** recent wins, problems, what you want from your manager

## Prompt

```text
Help me prepare for my 1:1 with [MANAGER].

Recent work and wins: [NOTES]
Problems or frustrations: [NOTES]
What I want from them (feedback, a decision, support, visibility, career talk): [NOTES]

Give me:
1. The 2-3 topics worth the time, in order. Status they can read elsewhere doesn't make the list.
2. How to raise the hardest topic, in two or three sentences, framed around a shared goal.
3. One specific question that would get me useful feedback.
4. Anything from my wins that should be written down for performance review time.
```
