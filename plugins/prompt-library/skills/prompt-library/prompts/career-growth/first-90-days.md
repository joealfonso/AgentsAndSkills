# First 90 days in a new role

**Use when:** you've accepted a new role and want to start well

**You'll need:** the role, team, what you know about expectations

## Prompt

```text
Build a 30-60-90 day plan for my new role: [ROLE] at [COMPANY].

What I know: [team, manager's priorities, known problems, what they hired me for]

Days 1-30, learn: who to meet (and the question to ask each), what to read, what to observe, and what not to change yet.
Days 31-60, contribute: one early win that's visible and low-risk, and the relationships it needs.
Days 61-90, lead: the larger problem I'll take on and how I'll propose it.

Add: a short message for my manager in week one confirming what success looks like at 90 days, and a list of the first 10 people to meet.
```
