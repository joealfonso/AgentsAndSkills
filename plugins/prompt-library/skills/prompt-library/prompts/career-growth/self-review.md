# Performance self-review

**Use when:** review season

**You'll need:** your brag doc or notes, the review questions, your level's expectations

## Prompt

```text
Help me write my self-review.

Review questions: [PASTE]
My notes or brag doc: [PASTE]
Expectations for my level: [PASTE or describe]

1. Pick the 3-4 accomplishments that best match my level's expectations. Explain the choice.
2. Answer each question: lead with the result, then my specific contribution, then evidence. "I" for my own work.
3. Growth areas: one or two real ones with what I'm already doing about them. Not fake weaknesses.
4. Flag claims a calibration committee would question for lack of evidence.

Confident and specific. No modesty padding, no inflation.
```
