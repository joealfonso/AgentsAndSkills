# Cut a case study in half

**Use when:** your case study is too long, too process-heavy, or buries the point

**You'll need:** the case study text

## Prompt

```text
Cut this case study to about half its length.

[PASTE]

Rules:
- Keep every decision, constraint and result. Cut process narration (research methods listed for their own sake, "we then held a workshop").
- Move the outcome to the top in one sentence.
- Flag every "we" where I'm describing my own decision, and fix it to "I".
- Keep my phrasing where it's specific. Only rewrite what's generic.
- Remove headings that exist only for structure.

Show the cut version, then list what you removed in one line each so I can put anything back.
```
