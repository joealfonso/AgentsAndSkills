# Research synthesis

**Use when:** you have interview notes or transcripts and need findings

**You'll need:** notes or transcripts, the research questions

## Prompt

```text
Synthesize these research notes.

Research questions: [LIST]
Notes/transcripts: [PASTE, labeled by participant]

1. Findings, each as one sentence a stakeholder could repeat, ranked by how much they affect the decision.
2. For each finding: how many participants support it (e.g. 4 of 6), 1-2 short quotes with participant label, and any participant who contradicts it.
3. Answers to each research question, or "not answered" with what's missing.
4. Surprises: things we didn't ask about but kept coming up.
5. Recommendations, separated from findings, each tied to the findings behind it.

Don't promote something one person said into a theme. Mark weak evidence as weak.
```
