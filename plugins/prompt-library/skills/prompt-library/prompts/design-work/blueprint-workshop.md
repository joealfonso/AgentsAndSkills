# Service blueprinting workshop

**Use when:** you're bringing people together to map a service, and need the right people and a plan that gets the backstage truth

**You'll need:** the service, who's available, time, in-person or remote

## Prompt

```text
Plan a service blueprinting workshop for [SERVICE / SCENARIO].

People available: [ROLES]
Time: [HOURS]. Format: [in person / remote, tool used]

1. Who must be in the room and why. Push for frontline staff and support agents, not only managers: they know how the backstage really works. Name any missing role that would leave a lane empty.
2. Prep: what evidence to gather beforehand (research, tickets, process docs, system lists) and a pre-filled customer-actions lane to start from.
3. Agenda with times: frame the scenario and its boundaries, walk customer actions, then frontstage, backstage, support, then mark fail points, waits and handoffs, then prioritize.
4. Facilitation moves for the hard moments: "that's how it should work, how does it actually work?", disagreements between teams, a step nobody owns, and the exception everyone forgot.
5. How to capture uncertainty and anomalies on the board (a distinct marker for assumptions, disagreements and one-off stories) so they aren't smoothed over.
6. Outputs and owners: the digitized blueprint, the ranked fail points, the open questions, and who follows up on each by when.
```
