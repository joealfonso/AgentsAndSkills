# Questions to ask them

**Use when:** the end of any interview round

**You'll need:** the role, who you're meeting and their title, what you still need to know

## Prompt

```text
I'm meeting [NAME, TITLE] for the [STAGE] round for [ROLE] at [COMPANY].

What I still need to know to decide (comp aside):
[e.g. how design decisions get made, whether AI work is real, team stability, what success looks like in year one]

Write 6 questions tailored to this person's role, not generic ones. For each:
- The question, short enough to say in one breath.
- What a good answer and a worrying answer would sound like.

Order them so the most important to my decision come first, since time often runs out. Skip anything answerable from their website.
```
