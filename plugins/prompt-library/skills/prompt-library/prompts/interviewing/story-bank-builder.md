# Story bank builder

**Use when:** you're preparing for interviews and want 8-10 reusable stories ready to go

**You'll need:** your resume, a few projects you're proud of or that went badly

## Prompt

```text
Help me build a story bank for design leadership interviews.

Resume:
[PASTE]

Projects I could talk about (a line or two each, including ones that went badly):
[LIST]

Step 1: Map my projects against the questions staff/principal designers get most: influencing without authority, a failed project, a disagreement with product or engineering, raising the quality bar, ambiguous problem framing, measurable impact, mentoring, shipping under constraints, AI or new technology, changing your mind from evidence. Show which project covers which question and where I have gaps.

Step 2: For the 8 strongest pairings, ask me for the missing details one story at a time (situation, the decision I personally made, what I chose not to do, the result, what I'd do differently). Wait for my answers.

Step 3: Write each story as a 90-second spoken answer: one sentence of context, the tension, my decision and why, the result with a number if real, one line of reflection. "I" for my work.

Don't write the stories in step 1. Don't invent results.
```
