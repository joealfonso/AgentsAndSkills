# Resume bullet rewrite: activity to impact

**Use when:** your resume describes what you did instead of what changed because of it

**You'll need:** your resume bullets, any metrics or outcomes you know

## Prompt

```text
Rewrite my resume bullets so each one leads with impact, not activity.

Bullets:
[PASTE BULLETS]

What I know about outcomes (numbers, adoption, time saved, decisions changed, even rough):
[PASTE, or "none"]

Rules:
- Pattern: result, then how, then scope. "Cut X from A to B by doing Y, across Z."
- Use only facts I gave you. Where a number would help and I didn't give one, write [METRIC?] and tell me what to look up.
- "I" work only. If a bullet describes team work, keep it honest ("led", "partnered with") rather than claiming it.
- Cut tool lists and process words (collaborated, leveraged, utilized, spearheaded).
- Max two lines per bullet.

Leave bullets that already state impact unchanged and mark them KEEP. For the rest, show before and after. End with the 2-3 [METRIC?] gaps that would most strengthen the resume.
```
