# Replying to a rejection

**Use when:** you got a no and want to keep the door open or learn something

**You'll need:** the rejection message, how far you got

## Prompt

```text
I was turned down for [ROLE] at [COMPANY] after [STAGE].

Their message:
[PASTE]

Write a short reply (under 70 words) that:
- Thanks them without groveling.
- Asks one specific question that could get real feedback, worded so it's easy to answer in a sentence (e.g. "Was there one area where another candidate was clearly stronger?").
- Leaves the door open for future roles if it's a company I'd still want.

Then, separately, give me two honest hypotheses for why it might have gone this way given the stage, and what I could test next time. Label them as guesses.
```
