# LinkedIn post from real work

**Use when:** you learned or shipped something worth sharing and want a post that doesn't sound like LinkedIn

**You'll need:** the thing that happened, the one point you want people to take away

## Prompt

```text
Turn this into a LinkedIn post.

What happened: [STORY, project, lesson, or observation]
The one point: [WHAT A READER SHOULD TAKE AWAY]
Anything confidential to leave out: [DETAILS]

Rules:
- First line is the point or a specific detail, not a hook formula ("I was wrong about...", "Unpopular opinion").
- 120-200 words. Short paragraphs, no bullet-point listicle.
- One concrete detail only I would know.
- No hashtags beyond 2, no emojis, no em dashes, no "Here's what I learned:", no closing question bait.
- End on the last concrete detail, not a moral.

Give two versions with different openings.
```
