# Conference talk proposal

**Use when:** submitting to a design, AI or product conference

**You'll need:** the talk idea, the conference and its audience, your evidence

## Prompt

```text
Write a talk proposal for [CONFERENCE], whose audience is [AUDIENCE].

The idea: [WHAT I WANT TO SAY]
Evidence and stories I have: [PROJECTS, DATA, EXAMPLES]
Length: [MINUTES]

Produce:
1. Three title options, specific and under 10 words, none of them puns.
2. An abstract under 150 words: the problem attendees have, the claim, what they'll leave with.
3. Three takeaways, each something an attendee could do on Monday.
4. A talk outline with time per section.
5. A short speaker bio for this audience.

Tell me plainly if the idea is too familiar for this audience, and what angle would make it new.
```
