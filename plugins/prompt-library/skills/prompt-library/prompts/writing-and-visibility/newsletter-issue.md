# Newsletter issue

**Use when:** writing a recurring note to your audience

**You'll need:** what you've read, made or noticed lately

## Prompt

```text
Draft a newsletter issue from these notes.

Notes: [LINKS with my reactions, things I made, observations]
Audience: [WHO SUBSCRIBES and why]

Structure: one lead item with my actual opinion (150-250 words), 3-5 short items with one line each on why it's worth their time, and one closing line that's a pointer, not a sign-off speech.

Every item needs my take. Drop links I didn't have a real reaction to.
```
