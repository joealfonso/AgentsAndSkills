# Bio in every length

**Use when:** you need a bio for a talk, a podcast, a site, or an intro

**You'll need:** your current role and focus, 2-3 proof points

## Prompt

```text
Write my bio in four lengths, third person unless I say otherwise.

Current focus: [WHAT I DO NOW]
Proof points: [2-3 specific achievements]
For: [EVENT / SITE / PUBLICATION and its audience]

1. One line (under 20 words).
2. Short (about 50 words).
3. Medium (about 100 words).
4. A spoken intro for a host to read aloud (about 40 words, easy to say).

Lead with what I do and for whom, not years of experience. No adjectives about myself ("passionate", "seasoned"). Only facts I gave you.
```
