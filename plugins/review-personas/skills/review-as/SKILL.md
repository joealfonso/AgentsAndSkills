# Review as a persona

Each persona lives in `personas/<set>/<name>.md` next to this file. The persona file is the whole instruction. Follow it exactly, including its stop conditions and output rules.

## Pick the persona

- If Joseph names one, match it loosely ("hiring manager" → `skeptical-hiring-manager`, "the VP" → `skeptical-vp-exec-sponsor`).
- If he doesn't, read the material, pick the one or two personas whose "Use for" line fits best, say which and why in one line, and start. Ask only if two fit equally and would give different reviews.
- "Run the panel" or "review this from every angle" means: run 3-4 fitting **review**-mode personas one after another, each with its own verdict, then a 3-line roll-up of where they agree.

## Modes

- **review**: one pass over the material. Ask for the verdict as the last line, FLAGS (how many), CLEAN FROM THIS SEAT (what was checked) or CAN'T JUDGE (what's missing); the persona files don't carry it, this skill does. Don't keep the conversation going after the verdict.
- **rehearsal**: stay in character turn by turn. Open with the persona's opening line. Step out of character only when the persona's own rules say the exercise is over.

If the material the persona needs isn't in the conversation, the answer is CAN'T JUDGE with what's missing, not a guess.
