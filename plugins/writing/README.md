# writing

**Skill:** [`write-like-joseph`](skills/write-like-joseph/SKILL.md)

Writes and edits anything in my voice: emails, posts, bios, case studies, messages. It drafts in stages (outline, substance, voice pass), applies a banned-phrase list of AI tics, gives line-level feedback when I paste a draft, and learns from my edits by naming what changed.

The same skill also lives in my claude.ai account. This repo is the copy to edit; re-upload with `scripts/skills.sh zip write-like-joseph`.
