# Offer comparison

**Use when:** you have one or more offers (or an offer versus staying) and need to compare them honestly

**You'll need:** each offer's details, your current situation, your priorities

## Prompt

```text
Help me compare these options.

Option A: [COMPANY, ROLE, LEVEL, base, bonus, equity (type, amount, vesting, strike or last valuation), sign-on, benefits, remote policy, start date]
Option B: [same]
Current job (if staying is an option): [same]

My priorities: [e.g. scope, AI work, stability, comp, growth to leadership]

1. Normalize comp into year-1 cash and 4-year total. Show the math. Treat private-company equity as a range (zero, a modest outcome, the stated valuation) instead of one number.
2. Compare non-comp factors against my priorities, citing what I told you. Mark unknowns.
3. List the questions I should ask each company before deciding.
4. Say which option looks strongest and why, and what would change that answer.

I'm not asking for financial advice, just help structuring the decision. Don't pick for me if it's genuinely close; tell me what it hinges on.
```
