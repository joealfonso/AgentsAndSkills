# Accepting or declining an offer

**Use when:** you've decided and need to tell them, or tell the other companies

**You'll need:** the decision, who you're writing to, anything to keep open

## Prompt

```text
Write a message [ACCEPTING / DECLINING] the offer for [ROLE] at [COMPANY], to [NAME].

Anything to confirm in writing (start date, title, comp terms): [DETAILS]
Do I want to keep the door open? [YES / NO]

Under 120 words. Gracious, direct, no long explanation of my reasoning. If declining, one sentence of reason at most, and nothing that could read as a negotiating move unless I say it is one.

If accepting, also draft a short note to withdraw from other active processes.
```
