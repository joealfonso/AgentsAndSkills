# Counter-offer email or script

**Use when:** you have an offer and want to negotiate it

**You'll need:** the offer, your target, your reasons and any leverage

## Prompt

```text
Help me negotiate this offer.

Offer: [DETAILS]
What I want: [e.g. base to X, more equity, sign-on to cover a bonus I'm leaving, title, start date]
Why it's justified: [market data, competing offer, scope of the role, what I'm leaving behind]
Who I'm talking to: [RECRUITER / HIRING MANAGER]

1. Pick the one or two asks most likely to move, and explain the order.
2. Write a short email (under 150 words) that is warm, specific, and states numbers instead of ranges. It should make clear I want the job.
3. Write a phone script for the same conversation: opener, the ask, and responses to "that's the top of the band," "we don't negotiate equity," and "what's your number."
4. Name my walk-away point in one sentence, based on what I told you, so I hold it on the call.

No apologizing, no over-explaining, no bluffing about offers I don't have.
```
