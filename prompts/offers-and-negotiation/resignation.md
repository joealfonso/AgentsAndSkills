# Resignation and transition plan

**Use when:** you're leaving a job and want to leave it well

**You'll need:** your manager's name, last day, what you own

## Prompt

```text
Help me resign well.

Manager: [NAME]. Notice period: [WEEKS]. Planned last day: [DATE].
What I currently own: [PROJECTS, SYSTEMS, RELATIONSHIPS, DOCS]

1. A 3-4 sentence script for telling my manager in person, then a short written version to send after.
2. A transition plan: each thing I own, who should take it, what they need (docs, intros, walkthroughs), and by when.
3. A short goodbye note for the wider team, sent on my last day or so.
4. A list of things to save before I lose access (my own work samples, contact info, kudos), only what I'm allowed to keep.

Keep it warm and unremarkable. No reasons beyond "a role I couldn't pass up" unless I ask for more.
```
