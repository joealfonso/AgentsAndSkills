# Design exercise or whiteboard prep

**Use when:** you have a live design challenge or take-home coming up

**You'll need:** the prompt (if known), format and time limit

## Prompt

```text
I have a [LIVE WHITEBOARD / TAKE-HOME / APP CRITIQUE] exercise for [ROLE] at [COMPANY]. Time: [LENGTH].
Prompt (if known): [PROMPT or "unknown"]

1. Give me a time-boxed plan for the session: how many minutes for clarifying questions, users and problem, constraints, ideas, a chosen direction, edge cases, and wrap-up.
2. List the 8 clarifying questions worth asking in the first 5 minutes, ordered by how much they'd change the design.
3. If the prompt is known: sketch two meaningfully different directions in words, and which I'd pick and why. If unknown: give me three practice prompts at this company's level and domain.
4. Name the traps: what interviewers usually mark candidates down for in this format.

Then offer to run a timed practice where you play the interviewer and only answer what I ask.
```
