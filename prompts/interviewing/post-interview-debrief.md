# Post-interview debrief

**Use when:** right after an interview, while it's fresh

**You'll need:** your raw notes or a voice memo transcript

## Prompt

```text
Debrief this interview with me. Raw notes:
[PASTE]

Give me:
1. What they seemed to care about most, with the moment that showed it.
2. Answers that landed and answers that didn't, with your read on why. Base it on what I wrote, not on guesses about their faces.
3. Anything I said that I should correct or expand on in the thank-you note.
4. Open questions about the role or team that I didn't get answered, ranked by importance.
5. One thing to do differently in the next round.

Then draft a thank-you note under 100 words that references one specific thing from the conversation. No "thank you for your time" opener.
```
