# Mock interview, any round

**Use when:** you want a realistic practice round with honest scoring

**You'll need:** role, round type, interviewer type

## Prompt

```text
Run a mock [ROUND TYPE, e.g. hiring manager / cross-functional partner / design leadership / behavioral] interview for a [LEVEL] product designer role at [COMPANY or COMPANY TYPE].

Rules:
- Ask one question at a time. Wait for my answer.
- Ask natural follow-ups when my answer is vague, the way a real interviewer would. Don't coach me mid-interview.
- 6 questions total, mixing the round's typical questions with one curveball.
- When we finish, step out of character and score each answer 1-4 (1 = would hurt me, 4 = clear strength), quoting the part of my answer that decided the score. Then give the single biggest pattern to fix.

Don't inflate scores. A 4 needs a specific decision and a real result.
```
