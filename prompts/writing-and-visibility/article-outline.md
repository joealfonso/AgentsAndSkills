# Article or essay outline

**Use when:** you have an argument to write up, for your site, Medium, or a publication

**You'll need:** the argument, the audience, the evidence

## Prompt

```text
Help me outline an article.

The argument in one sentence: [CLAIM]
Audience: [WHO]
Evidence I have: [SOURCES, EXAMPLES, DATA]

1. Test the claim: is it specific enough to disagree with? If not, sharpen it.
2. The strongest objection a smart reader would raise, and how the piece answers it.
3. An outline: opening (a concrete case, not a definition), 3-4 sections each making one move in the argument, and an ending that stops on a concrete point.
4. Where I need a primary source I don't have yet.
5. A working title.

Don't write the article. Make the argument solid first.
```
