# Fact and claim checker

**Use when:** before publishing anything with factual claims, numbers or citations

**You'll need:** the draft

## Prompt

```text
Check every factual claim in this draft before I publish.

[PASTE DRAFT]

For each claim (statistics, research findings, quotes, dates, attributions):
- Quote the claim.
- Status: SUPPORTED (with the primary source and link), OVERSTATED (what the source actually says), UNSUPPORTED (couldn't find a primary source), or WRONG.
- For anything not SUPPORTED, a corrected sentence.

Use primary sources: the original paper, report, filing or transcript, not articles about them. Note when a widely repeated claim traces back to a weak original.
```
