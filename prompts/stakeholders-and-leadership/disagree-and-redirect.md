# Disagreeing with a stakeholder productively

**Use when:** a stakeholder wants something you think is wrong, and you need to say so without losing them

**You'll need:** their request, your concern, the relationship and power dynamic

## Prompt

```text
A stakeholder wants something I think is a mistake. Help me handle it.

Their request: [WHAT THEY WANT]
Their likely reasons: [WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT]
My concern: [WHY IT'S A PROBLEM, with evidence]
Relationship: [peer / senior to me / exec, how much trust exists]

1. First, steelman their position. Is there a version where they're right?
2. Find the goal we share, and frame my concern in terms of their goal, not design principles.
3. Offer an alternative that gets them most of what they want, or a cheap test that settles it.
4. Write what I'd actually say in the meeting (under 80 words) and a follow-up message.
5. Decide in advance what I'll do if they still say no: disagree and commit, escalate, or document.
```
