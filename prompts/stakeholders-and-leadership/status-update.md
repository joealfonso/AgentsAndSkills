# Status update

**Use when:** weekly or project updates that people actually read

**You'll need:** what happened, what's next, risks, the audience

## Prompt

```text
Write a status update for [AUDIENCE] on [PROJECT].

What happened: [NOTES]
What's next: [NOTES]
Risks or blockers: [NOTES]
Do I need anything from them? [ASK or "no"]

Format: one-line headline (on track / at risk / off track and why), then "Done", "Next", "Risks", and "Need" if there's an ask. One line per item. Under 120 words.

Lead with anything that changed since last time. Don't list activity that didn't move the project.
```
