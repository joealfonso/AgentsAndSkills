# Meeting prep

**Use when:** an important meeting where you need an outcome, not just attendance

**You'll need:** the meeting purpose, attendees, what you want out of it

## Prompt

```text
Prepare me for this meeting.

Purpose: [WHY IT EXISTS]
Attendees and what each cares about: [LIST]
The outcome I want: [DECISION / ALIGNMENT / FEEDBACK / INFO]
Time: [MINUTES]

1. A short agenda with time per item, ending in the decision or next step.
2. My opening in two sentences.
3. For each attendee: their likely position and the concern I should address before they raise it.
4. The one thing that must be decided before we leave, and how I'll ask for it.
5. A follow-up message template recording decisions, owners and dates.
```
