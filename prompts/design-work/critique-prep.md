# Preparing for a design critique

**Use when:** before you bring work to crit, so you get useful feedback instead of opinions

**You'll need:** the work, its stage, what you're unsure about

## Prompt

```text
Help me prepare to present this work for critique.

The work: [DESCRIBE or link]
Stage: [exploration / converging / near final]
What I'm unsure about: [LIST]

1. Write a 60-second framing: the problem, the stage, and what kind of feedback I want and don't want right now.
2. Turn my uncertainties into 3 specific questions for the room.
3. Predict the 3 comments I'm most likely to get, and which deserve a real answer versus a "noted, out of scope."
4. Suggest one thing to cut from what I show, so the room focuses on the open questions.
```
