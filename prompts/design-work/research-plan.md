# Lean research plan

**Use when:** you need evidence before a decision and have limited time

**You'll need:** the decision to inform, timeline, access to users

## Prompt

```text
Plan research to inform this decision: [DECISION]

Timeline: [e.g. 2 weeks]. Access: [who I can reach and how]. Budget: [tools, incentives].

1. The 3 questions this research must answer, phrased so an answer changes what we do.
2. The cheapest method that answers each (existing data, support tickets, 5 interviews, a usability test, a survey), and why that one.
3. Who to talk to, how many, and how to recruit them this week.
4. A day-by-day schedule.
5. What I'll deliver and to whom, in a form they'll actually read.

Tell me if the decision doesn't need new research and existing evidence is enough.
```
