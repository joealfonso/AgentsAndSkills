# Rainbow spreadsheet setup

**Use when:** you're about to run a usability study and want the rainbow spreadsheet ready before the first session

**You'll need:** your study plan or goals, the tasks, participant list, the team's expectations

## Prompt

```text
Set up my rainbow spreadsheet for this study. The template has these tabs: Information & Goals, Participants, Tasks & Metrics, Observations & Assumptions, Notes, Post Interview.

Study plan or goals: [PASTE]
Tasks: [LIST, or "draft them"]
Participants: [IDs, segments, roles, session times; no names needed]
What the team expects to see: [HUNCHES, opinions, things stakeholders are sure of]

Produce, as tables I can paste into each tab:
1. Information & Goals: goals, why this study now, the decisions it informs, research questions, method, time period, segments we must cover, and who this sample will miss.
2. Participants: one column per participant ID with segment, role, experience level, tech literacy and session focus. Mark high-value participants with * and say why in one line. Flag if no participant is an edge case (power user, new user, assistive technology, unusual setup).
3. Tasks & Metrics: each task written as a realistic goal, not an instruction, with a one-line success definition. Use the scoring 2 = completed, 1 = completed with difficulty, 0 = failed.
4. Observations & Assumptions: turn every team expectation into a * row, written as an observable behavior ("*Users find export without help"), not an opinion. Add the assumptions the team didn't say out loud but that the design depends on.

Don't invent participant details. Leave unknown cells blank.
```
