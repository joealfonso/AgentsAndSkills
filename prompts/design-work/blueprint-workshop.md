# Service blueprinting workshop

**Use when:** you're bringing people together to map a service, and need the right people and a plan that gets the backstage truth

**You'll need:** the service and journeys, who's available, time, in-person or remote

## Prompt

```text
Plan a service blueprinting workshop for [SERVICE / JOURNEYS].

People available: [ROLES]
Time: [HOURS]. Format: [in person / remote, tool used]

1. Who must be in the room and why. Push for frontline staff and support agents, not only managers: they know how the backstage really works. Also the owners who'll fill the support systems lane (IT, Legal and Privacy, Facilities, Content, vendors). Name any missing role that would leave a lane empty.
2. Prep: gather the source (brief, research, tickets, process docs), agree the lead persona and phases for each journey in advance, and pre-fill the actions lane.
3. Board setup: the persona strip at the top, numbered phases left to right, and the lanes with their subtitles: actions, evidence and frontstage (split out physical evidence only if the product has physical touchpoints), backstage, support systems, risks and open questions, with the lines of interaction and visibility marked. Bring screenshots or flows of the current product to place in the evidence lane.
4. Agenda with times: walk the actions lane, then evidence and frontstage, then backstage, then support systems, then a risks pass phase by phase, then assign Open items.
5. Facilitation moves: "that's how it should work, how does it actually work?", disagreements between teams, a step nobody owns, and the edge case everyone forgot. Anything uncertain goes on the board tagged Assumed or Open instead of being smoothed over.
6. Outputs: the digitized blueprints, the risks with fallbacks, and every Open item with an owner and a date.
```
