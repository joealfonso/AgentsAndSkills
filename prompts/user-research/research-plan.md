# Lean research plan

**Use when:** you need evidence before a decision and have limited time

**You'll need:** the decision to inform, timeline, access to users

## Prompt

```text
Plan research to inform this decision: [DECISION]

Timeline: [e.g. 2 weeks]. Access: [who I can reach and how]. Budget: [tools, incentives].

1. The 3 questions this research must answer, phrased so an answer changes what we do.
2. The cheapest method that answers each (existing data, support tickets, 5 interviews, a usability test, a survey), and why that one.
3. Who to talk to, how many, and how to recruit them this week. Include 1-2 deliberate edge participants: power users, people who abandoned the product, assistive technology users, unusual setups or locales, whichever fits. Anomalies come from the edges, and a sample of typical users won't produce them.
4. Who this sample will miss. Name the user groups or situations the plan can't speak for, so findings aren't stretched to cover them later.
5. How surprises get captured: who takes notes, in what format, and where off-script observations go so they survive to synthesis.
6. A day-by-day schedule.
7. What I'll deliver and to whom, in a form they'll actually read.

Tell me if the decision doesn't need new research and existing evidence is enough.
```
