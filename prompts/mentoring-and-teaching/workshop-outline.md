# Workshop or class outline

**Use when:** teaching a session, running a team workshop, or giving a lunch-and-learn

**You'll need:** topic, audience, length, the outcome you want

## Prompt

```text
Outline a [LENGTH] session on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE].

By the end they should be able to: [OUTCOME, as something they can do]

1. Agenda with time per segment, no more than 15 minutes of me talking in a row.
2. One hands-on exercise using realistic material, with instructions and how long it takes.
3. The 3 ideas that must stick, each with an example.
4. A pre-read or nothing (prefer nothing), and a one-page takeaway.
5. How I'll know it worked: one check at the end.

Cut content until it fits the time with room for questions.
```
