# Feedback on a junior designer's work

**Use when:** reviewing a mentee's or report's work and you want feedback that teaches

**You'll need:** the work, their level, what they're trying to learn

## Prompt

```text
Help me give feedback on this work from a designer at [LEVEL].

The work: [DESCRIBE or paste]
What they're working on growing: [SKILL]
My raw reactions: [NOTES]

1. Sort my reactions into: must fix, worth discussing, and my taste (which I should mostly keep to myself).
2. For the must-fix items, turn each into a question that leads them to see it, plus the direct version if they don't.
3. Name one thing they did well, specifically enough that they'd repeat it.
4. Pick the one lesson from this review tied to their growth goal. Everything else is secondary.

Keep it to what they can act on this week.
```
