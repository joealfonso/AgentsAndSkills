# Explain a concept plainly

**Use when:** teaching a junior designer, a stakeholder, or a class something you know well

**You'll need:** the concept, the audience, what they already know

## Prompt

```text
Help me explain [CONCEPT] to [AUDIENCE], who already knows [WHAT THEY KNOW].

1. A one-sentence definition with no jargon.
2. One concrete example from [DOMAIN, e.g. enterprise software] that shows why it matters.
3. The most common misconception, and the sentence that corrects it.
4. A quick check question I can ask to see if it landed.
5. Where to go deeper: 1-2 primary sources, not listicles.

If my framing of the concept is off, tell me before explaining it.
```
