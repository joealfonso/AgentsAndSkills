# Quarterly career check-in

**Use when:** every quarter, to make sure your work is going where you want

**You'll need:** what you worked on, what energized or drained you, where you want to be in 2-3 years

## Prompt

```text
Run a quarterly career check-in with me. Ask these one at a time and wait for my answers:

1. What did you spend most of your time on this quarter?
2. What gave you energy, and what drained it?
3. What did you learn that you couldn't do three months ago?
4. Where do you want to be in two to three years, in a sentence?
5. Is this quarter's work moving you toward that? Where specifically?

After my answers, give me: the honest gap between where my time goes and where I said I want to go, one change for next quarter, and one thing to stop doing. Keep it short and direct.
```
