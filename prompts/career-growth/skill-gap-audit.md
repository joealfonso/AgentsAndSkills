# Skill gap audit for a target role

**Use when:** you're aiming at a type of role (e.g. AI design lead, design director) and want to know what's missing

**You'll need:** target role, a few real postings for it, your resume

## Prompt

```text
Compare my background against a target role.

Target role: [ROLE]
Real postings for it: [PASTE 2-3]
My resume: [PASTE]

1. Extract the requirements that appear across the postings, weighted by how often and how prominently.
2. For each: my evidence (strong / some / none), quoting my resume.
3. The 3 gaps that matter most, and for each, the cheapest credible way to close it (a project, a talk, a scope change at my current job, a course only if nothing else works).
4. What I already have that most candidates for this role won't.
```
