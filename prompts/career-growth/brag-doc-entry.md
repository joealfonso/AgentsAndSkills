# Weekly brag doc entry

**Use when:** end of each week, 5 minutes, so review season isn't archaeology

**You'll need:** what you did this week, rough notes

## Prompt

```text
Turn my rough notes into brag doc entries.

This week: [NOTES, messy is fine]

For each item worth keeping: one line with what I did, the effect, and the evidence (a link, a quote, a number, who noticed). Group by: impact, influence, quality bar, helping others.

Drop routine work that had no effect beyond being done. Mark items that would support a promotion case at the next level with [LEVEL-UP].
```
