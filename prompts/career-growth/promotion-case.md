# Promotion case

**Use when:** you're aiming for the next level and need to make the case, or help your manager make it

**You'll need:** the next level's expectations, your evidence

## Prompt

```text
Help me build a promotion case to [TARGET LEVEL].

Expectations for that level: [PASTE LADDER or describe]
My evidence: [PROJECTS, outcomes, feedback, examples of operating at that level]

1. Map each expectation to my strongest evidence, with a strength rating (clear / partial / missing).
2. For "missing" or "partial", what I could do in the next quarter to close it, specifically.
3. A one-page case my manager could take into calibration: summary, 3 examples of already operating at the level, impact, and peer signals.
4. The objection a skeptical committee member would raise, and how to answer it.

Be honest if the case isn't ready yet, and say what's missing.
```
