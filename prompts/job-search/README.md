# Job search

Finding roles worth applying to and getting in the door: decoding postings, rewriting resume bullets, outreach, referrals, follow-ups and company research.

Fill in the `[BRACKETS]` and paste a prompt into any assistant, with [about-me.md](../about-me.md) above it, or ask Claude to use it by name through the `prompt-library` skill.

| Prompt | Use when |
|---|---|
| [Following up on an application](application-follow-up.md) | it's been 1-2 weeks since you applied or interviewed with no word |
| [Company research brief](company-research-brief.md) | before an application, a first call, or any interview |
| [Job posting decoder](job-posting-decoder.md) | you find a posting and want to know what they actually want before deciding to apply |
| [Outreach to a recruiter or hiring manager](recruiter-outreach.md) | you want to reach out cold or warm about a specific role |
| [Asking a contact for a referral](referral-ask.md) | someone you know works at a company you're applying to |
| [Replying to a rejection](rejection-reply.md) | you got a no and want to keep the door open or learn something |
| [Resume bullet rewrite: activity to impact](resume-bullet-rewrite.md) | your resume describes what you did instead of what changed because of it |
| [Should I apply? scorecard](should-i-apply-scorecard.md) | you have several open roles and limited time, and need to decide where to spend it |

*This file is generated by `scripts/build.py` from the prompts' frontmatter.*
