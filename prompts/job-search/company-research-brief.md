# Company research brief

**Use when:** before an application, a first call, or any interview

**You'll need:** company name, role, the interview stage

## Prompt

```text
Build a one-page research brief on [COMPANY] for a [STAGE] conversation about [ROLE].

Cover, with sources linked for every factual claim:
1. What they sell, to whom, and how they make money. One paragraph.
2. Recent news from the last 6 months: launches, funding, layoffs, leadership changes, AI moves.
3. Their product and design: what the product looks like today, visible UX strengths and problems, design leadership names if findable, any public design writing.
4. Their AI story: what they've shipped, claimed, or announced, and how believable it is.
5. Likely pressures on the team this role sits in.
6. Three smart questions I could ask that show I did this work, each tied to something above.

Use primary sources (company site, filings, engineering or design blogs, interviews with leaders) over aggregators. If you can't verify something, leave it out rather than hedge it.
```
