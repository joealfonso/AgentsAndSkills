# Outreach to a recruiter or hiring manager

**Use when:** you want to reach out cold or warm about a specific role

**You'll need:** the role, the person's name and title, one real connection point

## Prompt

```text
Draft a short message to [NAME, TITLE at COMPANY] about [ROLE].

Connection point (how I know them, mutual contact, something they wrote or shipped):
[DETAIL, or "cold"]

Why I'm a fit, in my words:
[2-3 POINTS]

Rules:
- Under 90 words for LinkedIn, under 140 for email. Write the LinkedIn version unless I say email.
- First sentence is about them or the role, not me.
- One specific proof point from my background, not a summary of my career.
- One clear, small ask (a 15-minute call, or who to talk to). No "I'd love to pick your brain."
- No flattery, no "I hope this finds you well," no em dashes.

Give two versions: one direct, one warmer. Then tell me which you'd send and why in one line.
```
