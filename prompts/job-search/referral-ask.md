# Asking a contact for a referral

**Use when:** someone you know works at a company you're applying to

**You'll need:** the contact, how well you know them, the role link

## Prompt

```text
Help me ask [NAME] for a referral to [ROLE] at [COMPANY].

How well we know each other: [e.g. worked together 2019-2021 / met once at a conference / LinkedIn only]
Role link: [URL]

Write a message that:
- Makes it easy to say no. If we're not close, ask for advice about the team first and the referral second.
- Includes a 3-sentence blurb about me they can forward as-is, specific to this role.
- Attaches no guilt and no long backstory.
- Is under 120 words, plus the forwardable blurb.

If our connection is too thin for a referral ask, tell me, and write the advice-first version instead.
```
