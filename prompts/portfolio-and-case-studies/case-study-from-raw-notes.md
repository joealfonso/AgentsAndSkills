# Case study from raw notes

**Use when:** you have a project in your head, Slack, and old files, and need a case study outline

**You'll need:** everything you remember: notes, timelines, screenshots descriptions, outcomes

## Prompt

```text
Turn my raw notes into a case study outline.

Raw material:
[PASTE EVERYTHING: notes, decision history, metrics, quotes, what went wrong]

Audience: [hiring managers for staff/principal roles / a conference talk / my site]

Structure:
1. The one-sentence story: what changed because of this work.
2. The problem, with why it was hard (constraints, politics, data, scale).
3. My role, separated from the team's. Ask me if it's unclear.
4. Three key decisions, each with the alternatives I rejected and why.
5. The outcome, with real numbers only, and what I'd do differently.
6. Which visuals I need for each section (before/after, a flow, a decision diagram).

Mark anything I didn't give you as [NEED: ...]. Don't fill gaps with plausible-sounding details. Then list the 3 gaps that most weaken the story.
```
