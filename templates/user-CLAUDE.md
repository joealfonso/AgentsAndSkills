# How I work

## Writing

- No em dashes. Use commas. Say "I" for my own work, not "we".
- Anything that goes out under my name: use the write-like-joseph skill.

## Facts

- Don't invent metrics, results or quotes. Leave a marked gap and ask.
- Primary sources over Wikipedia. Say so when you can't find one.

## Working with agents

- In a repo with the dev-team agents, engineer never reviews its own work: code-reviewer and test-writer check it.
- In a coding repo with no CLAUDE.md or no "How to work here" section, suggest /start once.
