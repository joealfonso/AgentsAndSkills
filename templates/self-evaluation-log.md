# Self-evaluation log

One row each time you run a [persona](../personas/) on your own work. The verdicts are the point: after a few months the log shows which flags keep coming back, and review season starts from evidence instead of memory.

Copy this file somewhere you'll keep it (a doc, a note, the repo of the project). Add a row right after the review, while the flag is fresh.

## How to fill it in

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Date | The day you ran the review |
| Work | What you reviewed, specific enough to find again: "Checkout redesign case study, v3" |
| Persona | Who reviewed it: "Strict Senior Product Designer" |
| Verdict | Exactly as the persona ended: FLAGS, CLEAN FROM THIS SEAT, CAN'T JUDGE, or a rehearsal verdict like PASS, NOT YET, TEACHABLE |
| Top flag | The first flag in the persona's own words, or "none" |
| Pattern | A short tag for the kind of flag, so repeats are easy to count: `no-evidence`, `we-vs-i`, `hedging`, `buried-ask`, `missing-states`, `activity-not-impact` |
| Changed | What you changed because of it, or "kept, because..." when you disagreed |

## Every quarter

Five minutes, before the [career check-in](../prompts/career-growth/career-check-in.md):

1. Count the pattern tags. Any tag that shows up three or more times is a habit, not a one-off. Write it down as a growth area.
2. Find the CAN'T JUDGE rows. They usually mean the work went out without the context a reader needs. Note what was missing.
3. Copy the CLEAN verdicts on work that shipped into your [brag doc](../prompts/career-growth/brag-doc-entry.md). An independent pass is evidence.
4. Before a [self-review](../prompts/career-growth/self-review.md) or [promotion case](../prompts/career-growth/promotion-case.md), run the [Calibration Committee](../personas/reality-checks/calibration-committee.md) and log that too.
