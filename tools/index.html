Design review tools

Three ways to review a design, from fast to formal

Each one runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing. Pick by how much is at stake.

  1. 01

    Design Review Card

    10–15 minutes · any design, before build

    26 checks across purpose, states, edges, access, words and failure recovery, plus five AI questions when they apply. Produces a follow-up list, not a grade.

  2. 02

    AI Design Evaluation Rubric

    30-second gut-check, then 18 scored criteria · AI features

    Agency, transparency, honesty, equity, real friction reduction and failure design, with gate criteria and a ship verdict.

  3. 03

    AI Readiness Review (AIRR-1.0)

    Risk-tiered, 27 criteria · high-consequence AI or launch gates

    Evidence-backed criteria mapped to NIST AI RMF, the EU AI Act, Microsoft HAX, Google PAIR, ISO/IEC 42001 and WCAG, with a three-condition verdict.
