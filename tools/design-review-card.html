Design Review Card

A fast, repeatable pass over any design before it goes to build. Ten to fifteen minutes. Produces a follow-up list, not a grade.

How to use it. Walk the card with the designer, not at them. Mark each line as you go. The point is not to score the work, it is to find the questions nobody has asked yet, so mark generously and move fast. Anything marked ? lands in the follow-up list at the bottom, which is the actual output of the review.
