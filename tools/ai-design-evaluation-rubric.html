AI Design Evaluation Rubric

A working checklist for judging AI-generated or AI-assisted design output before it ships, a case study is built around it, or it goes in front of a user.

Developed from principles proven while designing the AI integration layer for a large-scale enterprise employee-management platform (4.8B+ documents/year, 67 countries), and generalized here for use on any AI-assisted feature. Joseph Alfonso — josephalfonso.com

Full rubric

Score each criterion 0 (not addressed), 1 (partially addressed), or 2 (fully addressed). Mark N/A if genuinely irrelevant to this feature, N/A items are excluded from scoring. Items flagged Gate will block a "ready to ship" verdict on their own if scored 0, regardless of total.
